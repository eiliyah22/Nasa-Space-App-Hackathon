Holistic Framework

The Three Pillars of Mauritian Coastal Stewardship

Connecting satellite science with sustainable livelihood and cultural memory.

1. Economic Pillar

Supporting sustainable artisanal fisheries, ecotourism operators, and blue carbon economies with real-time lagoon navigation safety and verified water clarity indicators.

  • Artisanal pirogue speed safety alerts
  • Coastal hotel beach erosion awareness
  • Blue carbon mangrove economic valuation

2. Environmental Pillar

Deciphering coastal geomorphology through NASA-ISRO NISAR L-band Synthetic Aperture Radar. Tracking soil moisture, ground subsidence, and coral reef fringe vulnerability.

  • L-band penetration through cloud cover
  • Soil moisture mapping at 200m resolution
  • Multi-temporal interferometric coherence

3. Social & Cultural Pillar

Reconnecting every Mauritian citizen with our maritime heritage: from Le Morne Maroons history to Grand Port naval heritage, passing through intergenerational fisherfolk lore.

  • Intergenerational storytelling & Kreol voices
  • Child-safe coastal heritage quest games
  • Volunteer recruitment for reef restoration
